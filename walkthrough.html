Sample score

A solid property, with one thing to watch: the roof. The structure underneath is sound. The roof is the part of this house that will decide the next renewal.

The score is built from the measured record — roof, foundation, walls, porch, deck and outbuildings — not from photographs, and not from a drive-by. The same record is used at underwriting, at renewal, and if a claim ever comes in.
