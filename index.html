Property capture for insurers

Price the risk you can see.

Most underwriting is done from photographs. InsurScan gives underwriters and adjusters the property itself — a complete three-dimensional record, captured by drone from above and LiDAR at ground level — and a score built from that record.

The problem

A photograph is not a record.

Roofs are priced from old photographs and satellite images. Foundations, walls, porches and outbuildings are judged from a drive-by, if they are judged at all. Two inspections of the same house rarely agree. When a claim arrives, the file is thinner than the loss.

Capture

Two passes. One record.

Every property is captured the same way, from both altitudes.

LiDAR, at ground level

The record

What your team receives.

One complete record per property. The same record at underwriting, at renewal, and if a claim ever comes in.

01

A three-dimensional record

The roof and the whole property from above, and the structure at ground level, measured — not a handful of snapshots.

02

A score

A score for the property, built from the measured record — to support pricing, and to flag risk that photographs miss.

03

A dashboard

An adjuster opens the property and sees what is actually being insured: roof, structure and outbuildings, in one place. See a sample dashboard.

The record belongs to the insurer that commissioned it. It is not sold, shared, or reused for anyone else.

Pilot

Start with your own book.

Do not decide on slides. Put InsurScan on properties from your own book, and let your underwriters judge the record against the files they use now.

  1. You choose the properties

    New business, renewals, the ones you are least sure about. If it has a roof and a foundation, it qualifies.

  2. InsurScan captures them

    Drone high, LiDAR low. Every property gets both passes, captured the same consistent way.

  3. Your underwriters work from the record

    One complete three-dimensional record per property — roof, foundation, structures — ready to underwrite from.

  4. You judge it on real data

    At the end of the pilot you do not have a sales deck. You have real properties, real scans, and your own underwriters’ verdict.

No platform contract, no lock-in. The pilot is the offer: a fair test on your own properties.

Questions

Straight answers.

Does this replace our underwriters? +

No. It replaces the guesswork in front of them. Your underwriters still make every decision — they make it from a complete record of the property instead of a few photographs.

What exactly does the underwriter receive? +

One complete record per property: the full aerial capture of the roof and property, plus ground-level LiDAR scans of the foundation, walls, porch, deck and outbuildings — captured consistently across the pilot.

Do you sell the data? +

No. The record is made for the insurer that commissioned it. InsurScan does not sell property records or personal information to data brokers or advertisers.

What do you capture? +

The exterior of the property — roof, foundation, walls, porch, deck and outbuildings. Drone above, LiDAR at ground level. No phone capture.

Who is this for? +

Smaller carriers and mutuals — the insurers whose books are the houses nobody is scanning today.

InsurScan is operated by 1001579039 Ontario Inc., based in Timmins, Ontario, Canada (“InsurScan”, “we”, “us”). This policy explains what information we collect, why, and who receives it.

1. This website

This website has no accounts, no contact forms, and no checkout. Visiting it does not require you to give us your name, email, or payment details, and we do not sell personal information. Like most websites, the services that host and deliver this site may process basic technical information such as your IP address and browser type in order to serve the page.

2. Information collected during a pilot or capture

When a carrier, mutual, or other client engages InsurScan, we collect and create only what is needed to deliver the service: property addresses and the list of properties supplied by the client for capture; drone aerial imagery and ground-level LiDAR scans, measurements, and related property records created during capture; names, business contact details, and correspondence for the client staff we work with; any information a client or property owner chooses to send us in connection with a capture. Capture is of the exterior of a property — roof, foundation, walls, porch, deck, and outbuildings. InsurScan does not use property scans for advertising.

3. How we use it

To plan and carry out drone and LiDAR captures; to produce and deliver one three-dimensional property record per property to the client that commissioned it; to communicate about the pilot, schedule captures, and provide support; to meet legal, regulatory, insurance, and drone-operation requirements.

4. Who receives it

Property records are delivered to the client that commissioned the capture — for example, the insurer or mutual whose properties were scanned — and to its underwriters for their underwriting use. We may share information with service providers only as needed to operate, store, or deliver the service, and where required by law or a lawful request. We do not sell property records or personal information to data brokers or advertisers.

5. Retention and security

We keep pilot records and related information for as long as needed to deliver the service, support the client’s record of the property, and meet legal obligations, after which it may be deleted or archived. We use reasonable administrative and technical measures for the size of our operation, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure.

6. Your rights

If your personal information is in our records, you may ask to see it, ask for a correction, or ask a question about how it is handled, subject to legal and contractual limits — including that a property record commissioned by a client may also form part of that client’s records.
