The problem

Underwriting is only as good as what somebody happened to photograph.

Inspections today are slow, expensive, and inconsistent — so carriers end up paying claims they shouldn’t, and missing risk they should have caught.

/ 01

The roof is a rumour

The single most expensive part of the house gets decided from old photos, satellite shots, and whatever could be seen from a ladder. Nobody has a real, current record of it.

/ 02

Ground level gets a drive-by

Foundation, walls, porch, deck, outbuildings — the parts that crack, rot, and fail first are the parts nobody documents up close.

/ 03

Every inspection tells a different story

Different inspector, different photos, different notes. There is no single record of the property that two underwriters can open and both rely on.
